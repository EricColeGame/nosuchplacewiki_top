### How do I restore my perception meter when out of calming consumables?
If your inventory is depleted of aromatic salts, seek out stable rooms. Stable chambers are identifiable by steady, warm overhead lighting (incandescent instead of fluorescent) and the complete absence of radio static. Standing in these rooms for 45 to 60 seconds naturally replenishes your sanity meter back to a safe baseline.

### Why do chalk marks disappear in certain corridors?
