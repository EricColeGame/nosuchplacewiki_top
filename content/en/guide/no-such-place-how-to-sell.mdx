## Common Selling Mistakes and How to Avoid Them

Even veteran players occasionally make careless trading mistakes that set their progress back several hours. One of the most frequent beginner pitfalls surrounding **no such place how to sell** routines is letting impulse drive your liquidation. 

Here are the most common trading mistakes to watch out for:

- **Selling Quest-Adjacent Curios**: Some items appear to be standard junk but trigger discovery logs or unlock trade dialogues with hidden NPCs. Always check item descriptions for unique background lore before hitting confirm.
- **Ignoring Weight-to-Value Ratios**: Hauling ten heavy steel beams back to an outpost for a handful of coins wastes valuable stamina. Prioritize lightweight, high-density assets such as microchips, optical sensors, and specialized chemicals.
- **Accidental Bulk Liquidation**: When using quick-sell hotkeys, lock valuable equipment in your primary inventory to avoid accidentally wiping out your favored tools or weapons.
- **Depleting Vendor Capital**: Selling a massive stack of items that exceeds the vendor's cash cap zeroes out your profit for any overflow goods. Always monitor the merchant's active wallet balance during the transaction.
- **Overlooking Regional Tariffs**: Isolated outposts surrounded by high-threat hazards often enforce steep buying fees or low offer prices. Travel to stabilized central settlements when unloading truly valuable hoards.

Developing clean inventory habits between expeditions minimizes these errors. Take thirty seconds after every run to lock critical loadout items, evaluate what you need for current crafting goals, and systematically offload the rest.

### How often do vendors reset their cash and inventory?
While individual enclave timings vary based on location and danger rating, community reports indicate that standard merchants restock their currency pool and inventory every 24 to 48 in-game hours, or immediately following specific environmental resets.

### What should I prioritize buying first with my sale proceeds?
