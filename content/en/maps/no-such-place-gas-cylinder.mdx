## Critical Survival Hazards and How to Avoid Them

Transporting explosive payloads through hostile territory introduces unique failure states. Understanding environmental triggers ensures your team does not lose valuable scavenged loot to an accidental detonation.

```
[ Incoming Fire / Shrapnel ] 
            +                ---> [ Tank Rupture (0 HP) ] ---> [ Detonation: 8.5m Fatal Radius ]
[ High Drops (>2 meters)   ] 
```

Keep the following safety protocols in mind during transit:

* **Beware of Environmental Fire**: Steam vents, active electrical arcs, and burning debris can heat the cylinder rapidly. If the surface temperature meter on the tank begins beeping, drop it immediately and retreat to safe distance.
* **Watch Your Drop Distance**: Dropping the cylinder down elevator shafts or off catwalks higher than two meters will compromise its hull integrity. Always climb down ladders first and retrieve the cylinder from the upper deck if mechanics allow, or take the stairs.
* **Watch Corner Collision**: When rounding tight corners in the maintenance corridors, keep your camera centered. Scraping the tank against rough concrete walls produces loud acoustic pings that alert nearby patrols.
* **Avoid Ricochet Zones**: If caught in a firefight while hauling, drop the vessel behind reinforced steel barriers. Stray pistol rounds or shotgun pellets can pierce the casing and incinerate your entire squad.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where is the most consistent spawn for the no such place gas cylinder?
Based on player experience and aggregated run logs, the most reliable spawn point is the chained welding crib inside the Sub-Level 2 Maintenance Workshop, closely followed by Shipping Container B-07 in the Sector B Logistics Depot.

### Can a no such place gas cylinder explode while in a player's inventory?
Yes. If an enemy shoots your backpack or hits your character with high-caliber rounds while you are carrying the cylinder, the container can take damage. Once its 100 HP durability pool depletes, it detonates after a short hiss, dealing massive lethal damage to the carrier and nearby allies.

### How many cylinders are needed to power the emergency evacuation lift?
Powering the emergency lift requires two complete fuel vessels. Squads should plan their routes to clear the path between the storage depots and the lift shaft before retrieving both canisters to minimize time spent encumbered in high-traffic zones.

### What should I do if the no such place gas cylinder catches fire?
If the cylinder takes thermal damage or begins venting pressurized flame, release it instantly and sprint at least 10 meters away around hard corner cover. You will have approximately three seconds before catastrophic failure obliterates everything within its blast radius.
