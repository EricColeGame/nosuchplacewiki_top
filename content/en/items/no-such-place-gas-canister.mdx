## Step-by-Step Guide: Siphoning and Refilling Fuel Safely

Securing an empty canister is only the first half of the equation; filling it requires interacting with stationary fuel sources without alerting nearby hostiles or triggering accidental ignitions.

```
       [Secure Area] 
             │
             ▼
   [Equip Siphon Kit] 
             │
             ▼
[Target Fuel Reservoir] 
             │
             ▼
[Monitor Noise & Vapors] 
             │
             ▼
 [Stow & Balance Weight]
```

1. **Sweep and Clear the Vicinity:** Engine bays and commercial fuel tanks often generate ambient noise during extraction. Eliminate wandering hostiles within a 30-meter radius before initiating transfer.
2. **Inspect Container Integrity:** Check your canister durability. Attempting to fill a damaged container with low durability results in fuel loss and leaves a lingering scent trail that attracts stalkers.
3. **Equip the Siphon Tooling:** Place a siphon hose or manual pump into your active utility slot. Ensure the target vehicle or tank contains harvestable liquid.
4. **Initiate Transfer:** Hold your interaction key steady. Watch the volumetric dial fill to exactly 100%. Interrupting the process early preserves partial fuel, but wastes time if forced to reposition.
5. **Secure the Cap and Stow:** Immediately seal the canister to prevent vapor dispersal. Distribute the heavy container toward the center of your inventory to minimize lateral balance penalties during movement.

According to veteran survival documentation across the [Steam Community Guides](https://store.steampowered.com/), failing to carry a functional siphon kit is the number one reason novice scavengers return from logistics runs empty-handed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where is the most consistent place to find a no such place gas canister early in the game?
The Service Station 09 depot and nearby highway roadside wrecks offer the highest initial drop rate. Search inside maintenance bays, trunk compartments of delivery vans, and behind utility sheds near the highway off-ramp.

### Can a damaged gas canister be repaired, or should I scrap it?
Canisters with degraded durability can be patched using industrial sealant or metal scrap at an advanced workbench. If the structural integrity falls below 15%, player experience suggests recycling the canister into raw sheet metal to avoid dangerous fuel spills.

### Does fuel evaporate if a canister is stored without a cap?
Yes. Unsealed containers or improvised plastic jugs left in outdoor environments lose volume over time and release volatile vapors. Store your fuel inside sealed steel lockers or climate-controlled interior rooms to prevent volume loss.

### How many canisters should I keep stored for long-term safehouse survival?
A conservative operational reserve is four full units: two dedicated to defensive grid cycles, one reserved for emergency medical or purification needs, and one kept empty for opportunistic scavenging runs. Maintaining this balance ensures you never get caught without baseline power.
