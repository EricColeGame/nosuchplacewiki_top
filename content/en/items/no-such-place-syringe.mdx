## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you use the no such place syringe while sprinting?
Standard mechanics prevent a full sprint during the administration animation. However, your movement does not stop completely; your character drops into a steady jogging pace. Executing a crouch-slide immediately prior to use allows you to retain forward momentum while the injection finishes.

### Where is the most consistent spot to find a no such place syringe early on?
Player experience consistently highlights the wall-mounted first aid kits inside the Clinical Quarantine Wing as the most reliable early-game source. Checking bathroom medicine cabinets and officer lockers around Security Checkpoint Bravo also yields dependable early supplies.

### Do syringes expire or spoil inside your inventory?
Restorative injectors do not have an expiration timer or degradation meter once stored in your inventory or storage containers. You can stockpile them safely across long campaigns without worrying about biochemical breakdown or diminished potency.

### How many syringes should I keep in my active inventory loadout?
For standard exploration missions, carrying two or three injectors in a single stacked slot provides adequate emergency protection without burdening your bag space. During boss encounters or incursions into deep anomaly zones, increasing your reserve to two full stacks (six units) is highly recommended.
