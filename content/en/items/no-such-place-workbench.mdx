## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I relocate my no such place workbench after placing it?
Yes. You can dismantle the workstation using a standard demolition hammer. Provided the bench is at full durability when disassembled, you will recover 80% to 100% of the core materials used to build it. Always empty the internal queue and adjacent storage before deconstruction.

### How do I repair military-spec weapons using the no such place workbench?
Repairing high-tier military armaments requires at least a Tier 3 workstation equipped with a Precision Caliper attachment. Attempting to repair advanced firearms on a Tier 1 or Tier 2 setup will cap maximum item durability and risk permanently destroying weapon attachments.

### Why is my no such place workbench showing a "Shelter Incomplete" warning?
This status triggers whenever the station detects missing walls, incomplete ceilings, or excessive exposure to wind and precipitation. Ensure your structure features fully enclosed walls, a solid roof, and a functional doorway within a four-tile radius.

### Does placing multiple workbenches speed up crafting times?
Having multiple workbenches allows you to queue separate fabrication tasks simultaneously, which is ideal for cooperative multiplayer bases or large-scale ammo manufacturing. However, individual recipe crafting speed is governed by station tier, lighting, and passive upgrade modules rather than the sheer number of benches present.
