## Frequently Asked Questions

### What causes my reactor pod to consume fuel when my machines are turned off?
Even when external machines are idle, baseline life support, internal diagnostic monitors, and battery charging draw passive current from the grid. Additionally, if the unit remains set to Nominal or Overdrive mode rather than Standby, it burns a fixed baseline rate of fuel simply keeping the reaction chamber at operating temperature.

### Can the no such place reactor pod explode if overheated?
Yes. If internal core temperatures cross critical failure thresholds and automated scrams fail, containment failure will trigger a violent thermal explosion. This blast demolishes adjacent structural walls, irradiates the immediate surrounding area, and destroys any equipment wired directly to the main bus.

### How do I safely clear accumulated slag from the reaction chamber?
Before clearing slag, toggle the reactor terminal to Emergency Purge or Standby mode and allow the internal temperature to cool below 100°C. Once safe, open the front maintenance hatch using an engineering wrench and clear the slag deposits using a vacuum extractor or insulated field shovel.

### Why is my power output fluctuating erratically during operation?
Erratic power spikes or dips usually indicate either an unstable magnetic containment coil (Diagnostic Code ERR-FLUX-04) or a loose primary bus conduit. Inspect your junction relays and verify that the power draw of your connected machinery does not exceed the maximum rated output of your current operating mode.
