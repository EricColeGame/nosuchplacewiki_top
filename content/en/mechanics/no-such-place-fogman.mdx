## Rescue and Extraction Tactics at the Sacrificial Poles

If a squad member is knocked unconscious and hauled away, you enter a strict countdown. The swarm deposits living victims onto elevated wooden poles, initiating a ritual feeding cycle that gradually damages limb health until the character expires.

Executing a successful **no such place fogman** rescue operation requires precise timing, distraction tactics, and high lockpicking proficiency.

### The Phased Extraction Playbook

1. **Locate the Active Deathyard**: Use tactical camera panning to track the swarm carrying your companion. Feeding yards are marked by clustered totems and distinctive wailing audio cues.
2. **Deploy the Distraction Unit**: Position your fastest runner on the opposite flank. Fire a ranged shot or break stealth to pull the warrior cadre away from the central totems.
3. **Execute the Infiltration**: Send your primary infiltrator into the clearing while the camp defenders give chase.
4. **Pick the Lock Rapidly**: Shackles at the poles feature varying lock difficulties. If lockpicking fails, brute-forcing the lock generates noise that alerts nearby stragglers.
5. **Carry the Injured**: A rescued survivor often suffers from crippled legs. Ensure your rescuer possesses adequate strength to avoid encumbrance penalties while carrying the casualty to high ground.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does the term "no such place fogman" actually mean to players?
In survival and sandbox gaming communities, the phrase refers to the misconception that there is a secure, unpatrolled sanctuary inside mist-bound valleys. The reality is that swarm spawners encompass nearly every quadrant of these biomes, making true safety non-existent without heavily fortified walls.

### How fast do my characters need to be to outrun swarm warriors?
To comfortably outpace swarm warriors and prevent pursuit attacks from behind, your characters should maintain an unencumbered movement speed of at least 23 to 25 mph. Wearing wooden sandals and stripping off heavy backpacks will help hit this benchmark.

### Can you farm the leaders of the mist swarms for currency?
Yes. Hive Princes regularly spawn alongside feeding rituals. Taking down a Prince allows you to loot their heads, which command high bounties at regional trade stations. However, attempting this requires a disciplined squad capable of holding off endless reinforcements while securing the target.

### Is it safe to build an automated mining camp in the fog?
Automated outposts are prone to disaster unless your gates are manned by high-skill gunners on raised platforms. Without continuous oversight, pathfinding hitches can trap worker units outside the perimeter, leading to immediate capture by roving patrols.
