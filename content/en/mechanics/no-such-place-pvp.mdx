## Advanced Combat Tactics and Movement Techniques

Once you understand basic weapon swings and terrain layouts, surviving high-tier encounters requires refined movement mechanics. Advanced maneuvers such as slide-dodging, feathering sprint toggles, and baiting commitment animations distinguish experienced duelists from novices.

One of the most reliable strategies involves "range baiting." By stepping deliberately into an opponent's striking perimeter and instantly back-stepping, you trigger their swing animation without absorbing damage. During their recovery frames, close the distance to deliver an unblockable retaliatory combo. This bait-and-punish rhythm forms the bedrock of competitive dueling.

Verticality can also be weaponized. Dropping from pipes or ledges to deliver aerial drop-attacks catches opponents completely off-guard, delivering maximum poise damage and frequently knocking targets down. Always pair aggressive maneuvers with active stamina management; never initiate an offensive sequence if your remaining endurance cannot support an emergency roll.

## Surviving Ambush Encounters and Extraction Clashes

Extraction zones represent the most dangerous phase of any run, attracting opportunistic ambushers seeking easy spoils. Skirmishes in these areas are high-stakes, as losing here means forfeiting every scrap of gear accumulated throughout the expedition.

When approaching an extraction beacon, assume the surrounding perimeter is monitored. Never rush straight toward the activation terminal. Instead, perform a deliberate sweep of elevated catwalks, dark recessed alcoves, and nearby blind corners. Tossing a utility grenade into popular camping perches often forces hidden players to reveal themselves prematurely.

If an opponent ambushes you while you are initiating extraction, do not panic and commit to a slugging match with depleted health. Immediately break line of sight, deploy defensive utility such as smoke or flash canisters, and reset the engagement on equal footing.

Effective tactics for surviving extraction include:

*   **Staggering Terminal Activation:** Trigger the extraction timer, then immediately rotate to a concealed defensive vantage rather than standing out in the open.
*   **Baiting Third Parties:** If two separate hostile factions engage near the exit, hold your fire. Allow them to whittle down each other's health and resources before cleaning up the survivors.
*   **Resource Rationing:** Always reserve at least one full medical kit and a burst of stamina-recovery consumable specifically for the final sprint to the escape threshold.
*   **Audio Masking:** Use ambient machinery, such as activating noisy generators or steam vents, to mask your approach and silent flanking maneuvers.

Applying these extraction strategies ensures you consistently protect your character progression and valuable findings against predatory campers.
