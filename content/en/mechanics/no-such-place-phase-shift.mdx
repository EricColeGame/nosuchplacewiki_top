## Frequently Asked Questions

### What causes an emergency phase collapse during exploration?
An emergency phase collapse occurs when your internal resonance meter reaches zero while you remain in the shifted reality. When the meter empties, the game forcibly ejects your character back into the baseline dimension, inflicting moderate disorientation and a temporary cooldown penalty on your abilities.

### Can enemies follow through a no such place phase shift?
Most baseline enemies cannot follow you across dimensions. However, dual-woven entities can sense spatial tears created by a **no such place phase shift** and will navigate around physical barriers to intercept where they predict you will re-emerge.

### How do I reduce the cooldown on my no such place phase shift?
You can reduce your cooldown by equipping kinetic recovery gear, discovering hidden resonance shrines throughout the map, or collecting passive calibration modules that reduce startup and recovery frame delays.

### Are items collected in the shifted plane permanent?
Yes. Key items, story artifacts, and resource containers gathered while in the shifted plane stay in your inventory after you return to baseline reality, provided you reach a secure checkpoint before suffering fatal damage.
