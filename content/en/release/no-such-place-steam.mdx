### Recommended Graphical Adjustments for High Framerates

To achieve an optimal balance between visual fidelity and performance, consider tailoring your internal graphics settings using the following profile:

- **Shadow Resolution:** Lower from Ultra to High. In dim, ambient hallways, medium-high shadows yield negligible visual difference while reclaiming up to 12% in rendering overhead.
- **Volumetric Lighting / Fog:** Set to Medium. Ambient fog heavily taxes shader units; dropping this single setting often stabilizes erratic frame pacing.
- **Ambient Occlusion:** Keep enabled (SSAO or HBAO). Contact shadows ground the minimalist furniture and architectural beams, preventing the world from appearing flat.
- **Upscaling Technologies:** If using an RTX card, enable DLSS on "Quality" mode. For AMD or non-RTX systems, FSR provides equivalent headroom for higher base resolutions.
