## Essential Beginner Tips and Progression Routes

Starting out can feel overwhelming when resources are tight and the environment provides few overt tutorials. Based on player experience gathered across initial launch sessions, surviving your first several expeditions requires a conservative mindset. Rushing forward into unexplored sectors without adequate supplies frequently results in frustrating casualty loops.

To establish a solid operational foundation during your initial hours, prioritize safety and infrastructure over aggressive exploration:

1. **Establish a Primary Waypoint Network**: Do not venture past visual range of your starting haven until you have crafted basic navigational flares or signal beacons. Landmarks can look deceptively similar under adverse weather conditions.
2. **Prioritize Backpack and Storage Upgrades**: Raw materials occupy valuable grid space. Expanding your immediate carrying capacity allows you to bring back metal scrap and medical components in a single trip.
3. **Master Noise Discipline**: Hostile entities react aggressively to sprinting on hard surfaces like metal grating or loose gravel. Crouch-walk whenever navigating interiors.
4. **Maintain Backup Power Cells**: Your portable light sources and environmental scanners share battery reserves. Always reserve at least one charged power cell exclusively for your journey home.
5. **Log Anomalous Phenomena**: Inspecting unique artifacts provides research points that unlock passive perks at your workbench, accelerating your character progression.

## Performance Optimization and PC Requirements

Like many independent releases built on modern 3D engines, early builds frequently feature unoptimized shader compilation, uneven frame pacing, and sporadic memory consumption. Maximizing performance in **no such place early access** requires tuning graphical settings to prevent sudden frame drops during intense weather transitions or crowded interior sequences.

The table below outlines recommended configuration benchmarks alongside targeted graphical adjustments for steady framerates.

| Hardware Tier | Target Resolution / FPS | Recommended Graphical Presets |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Entry-Level Rig** (GTX 1660 / RX 580, 8GB RAM) | 1080p @ 30–45 FPS | Shadows Low, Volumetrics Off, Textures Medium, FSR Balanced |
| **Mid-Range Rig** (RTX 3060 / RX 6600, 16GB RAM) | 1080p–1440p @ 60 FPS | Shadows Medium, Ambient Occlusion High, Textures High, DLSS Quality |
