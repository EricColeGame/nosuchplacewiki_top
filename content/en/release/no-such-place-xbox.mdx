## Setting Up Your Audio and Display for Maximum Immersion

Because this title relies heavily on psychological atmosphere, setting up your television or gaming monitor properly makes a measurable difference. Playing with incorrect black levels or crushed dynamic ranges can turn an eerie, artfully lit corridor into an unplayable block of muddy pixels.

Take five minutes before jumping into your first session to calibrate your system:

*   **Calibrate HDR on Xbox:** Navigate to `Settings > General > TV & display options > Calibrate HDR`. Follow the on-screen prompts so contrast limits correctly reflect your display's peak nits.
*   **Audio Format Selection:** If you are using headphones, activate Windows Sonic for Headphones or Dolby Atmos for Headphones in your console sound settings. This enhances positional audio cues significantly.
*   **Variable Refresh Rate (VRR):** If your television supports HDMI 2.1 VRR, keep it enabled. It smooths out any minor frame timing hitches during intense lighting transitions.

Taking these preliminary steps ensures your time with **no such place xbox** looks and sounds exactly as the creative team intended, drawing you into its haunting architecture without technical distractions.
